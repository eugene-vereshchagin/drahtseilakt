# Looks

The **Look** line in *Options, Graphics* chooses how levels are drawn. It changes only the picture. Physics, runs,
replays and records stay exactly the same.

| Look | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Original** (classic) | rounded shapes with the textures of your own copy of Ballance (needs the copy) |
| **Remaster** | the same rounded shapes with the toolbox's enlarged 4K textures and a little relief (needs `drahtseilakt-toolbox remaster`) |
| **Drahtseilakt** | our own textures on rounded shapes, shipped with the game. The default |
| **Own pack** | a texture pack of yours from the `textures/` folder in your data folder |

The next level you start uses the new Look.

## Rounding

The original's shapes are made of hard angles. Drahtseilakt rounds the curved ones, such as balls, pipes and arches,
on every map, also on community maps that never had it. Box edges stay sharp. A map the toolbox has prepared brings its
rounding with it; any other map is rounded as it loads. The rounding is only a picture: the ball rolls on the
original's shapes.

## Back to 2004

If you want it as it was, switch on the mod **Back to 2004** (*Options, Extended, Mods*; see [Mods](mods.md)).
It turns off the rounding and the enlarged textures, so levels are drawn as their authors made them, and it also gives
you a low resolution blown up into hard pixels in a 4:3 frame, textures that shimmer in the distance and, if you wish,
a frame cap. The run itself stays the same, and replays play back identically.
