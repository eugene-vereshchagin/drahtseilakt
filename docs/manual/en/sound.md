# Sound and music

*Options, Sound* sets the volume of the music and of the sounds (rolling, hits, the fall, the menu's clicks) from 0 to
10, and the **sound theme**: where the sounds and music come from.

| Theme | What it is |
|---|---|
| **neuro** | sounds and music made by an AI for this game, synthesized from scratch. The default |
| **system** | recordings with open licences by several authors; who made what is listed in the game's credits |
| **original** | the sounds and music of your own copy of Ballance, made into a theme by the toolbox on your machine (see [The toolbox](toolbox.md)); it appears only after that |
| your own | a theme folder inside `sounds/` in your data folder; what it lacks is taken from another theme |

The sounds of the original are never part of the game:
they stay with your copy.
