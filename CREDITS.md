## The owner's picks (the Drahtseilakt set)

Thirteen more community maps, picked by the project's owner, make up the Drahtseilakt set (Start > Drahtseilakt); the game has no levels of its own beyond its test and showcase levels. **They belong to their authors and are shipped converted to Drahtseilakt's open format (from each author's `.nmo`, with our own textures where the map's own are not embedded; nothing of the original game is included: where a map carried a picture of the original game, ours stands in, and the map is marked "proprietary textures removed"), with thanks; if an author objects, we will remove the map.** Each folder's `level.txt` names the author and where the map was posted; where no post is known, the author is credited by name and the video the map was found in is given. ## Community maps shipped with the game

Thirteen maps made by players of Ballance are shipped in `assets/levels/community/` and listed in the game under Start > Community picks (in the classic and the extended mode), in the order below: the catalogue's difficulty rises from 1 to 5. **These maps belong to their authors; they are shown here with thanks. If an author objects, we will remove the map.** (The same notice is shown in the game's menu and Credits screen, in every language of the interface.) The maps are the authors' files, unmodified except that the pictures of the original game five of them carried are replaced by ours (marked "proprietary textures removed"; THIRD-PARTY.md, "Levels"); each folder's `level.txt` names the author, the version, the date posted and the source. They are not covered by the game's licence.

Source of the list: the community catalogue [BallanceCustomMaps](https://github.com/pseudoacetyl/BallanceCustomMaps/blob/main/CustomMapList.md) by pseudoacetyl (MIT). Dates are the catalogue's "Posted"; difficulty is the catalogue's (1 easiest, 5 hardest).
