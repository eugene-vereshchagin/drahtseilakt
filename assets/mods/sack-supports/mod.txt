# Sack supports: the swinging sacks' holders, which float in the air in the original, hang on something: a stone
# column, a striped mast, a timber post, a gate of two posts beside the track, or a beam to a wall that stands near.
# The game chooses for each sack, as the level loads, what stands clear of the ball's way, of every moving part and
# of the camera's sight (crates/game/src/supports.rs); where nothing does, the sack hangs as it always did.
# Only the look changes (the game's own parts have the pieces; the original's models stay as they are): nothing is
# added to the physics, so the run stays classic and replays play the same.
