# Back to 2004: undoes our rendering fixes, so the game looks as it did on a PC of the year of its release: a low
# resolution scaled up with hard pixels in a 4:3 frame, textures without mipmaps or anisotropy (the distant shimmer),
# a frame cap and, if you dare, 16-bit colour. Only the look changes; the run stays classic, replays play the same.
