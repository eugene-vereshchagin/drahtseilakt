# Physics lab (after PhysicsEditor): gravity as a vector (down, and sideways along the world's x and z) and the
# physics' time factor as options. A rule variant: the run is
# marked modded and its replay names this mod with the values, so it plays back the same.
name.en Physics lab
name.ru Физическая лаборатория
name.de Physiklabor
name.zh 物理实验室
name.fr Labo de physique
name.es Laboratorio de física
name.it Laboratorio di fisica
description.en Gravity, down and sideways, and the physics' time factor (originals: 20, 0 and 2.0).
description.ru Гравитация вниз и вбок и множитель времени физики (в оригинале 20, 0 и 2,0).
description.de Schwerkraft nach unten und seitlich, Zeitfaktor der Physik (Original: 20, 0 und 2,0).
description.zh 可调向下与侧向重力及物理时间倍率（原版为 20、0 与 2.0）。
description.fr Gravité vers le bas et latérale, facteur de temps physique (originaux : 20, 0 et 2,0).
description.es Gravedad hacia abajo y lateral, factor de tiempo físico (originales: 20, 0 y 2,0).
description.it Gravità verso il basso e laterale, fattore di tempo fisico (originali: 20, 0 e 2,0).
author Drahtseilakt
version 1.0
licence GPL-3.0-only
requires 0.1
option gravity number 0 60 2 20
option gravity_x number -20 20 1 0
option gravity_z number -20 20 1 0
option time number 5 40 1 20
label gravity en Gravity
label gravity ru Гравитация
label gravity de Schwerkraft
label gravity zh 重力
label gravity fr Gravité
label gravity es Gravedad
label gravity it Gravità
label gravity_x en Gravity sideways X
label gravity_x ru Гравитация вбок X
label gravity_x de Schwerkraft seitlich X
label gravity_x zh 侧向重力 X
label gravity_x fr Gravité latérale X
label gravity_x es Gravedad lateral X
label gravity_x it Gravità laterale X
label gravity_z en Gravity sideways Z
label gravity_z ru Гравитация вбок Z
label gravity_z de Schwerkraft seitlich Z
label gravity_z zh 侧向重力 Z
label gravity_z fr Gravité latérale Z
label gravity_z es Gravedad lateral Z
label gravity_z it Gravità laterale Z
label time en Time factor x10
label time ru Время физики x10
label time de Zeitfaktor x10
label time zh 时间倍率 x10
label time fr Facteur de temps x10
label time es Factor de tiempo x10
label time it Fattore tempo x10

do world.gravity opt.gravity
