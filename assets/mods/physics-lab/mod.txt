# Physics lab (after PhysicsEditor): gravity and the physics' time factor as options. A rule variant: the run is
# marked modded and its replay names this mod with the values, so it plays back the same.
name.en Physics lab
name.ru Физическая лаборатория
name.de Physiklabor
name.zh 物理实验室
name.fr Labo de physique
name.es Laboratorio de física
name.it Laboratorio di fisica
description.en Gravity and the physics' time factor to try (originals: 20 and 2.0).
description.ru Гравитация и множитель времени физики (в оригинале 20 и 2,0).
description.de Schwerkraft und Zeitfaktor der Physik (Original: 20 und 2,0).
description.zh 可调重力与物理时间倍率（原版为 20 与 2.0）。
description.fr Gravité et facteur de temps physique (originaux : 20 et 2,0).
description.es Gravedad y factor de tiempo físico (originales: 20 y 2,0).
description.it Gravità e fattore di tempo fisico (originali: 20 e 2,0).
author Drahtseilakt
version 1.0
licence GPL-3.0-only
requires 0.1
option gravity number 0 60 2 20
option time number 5 40 1 20
label gravity en Gravity
label gravity ru Гравитация
label gravity de Schwerkraft
label gravity zh 重力
label gravity fr Gravité
label gravity es Gravedad
label gravity it Gravità
label time en Time factor x10
label time ru Время физики x10
label time de Zeitfaktor x10
label time zh 时间倍率 x10
label time fr Facteur de temps x10
label time es Factor de tiempo x10
label time it Fattore tempo x10

on start
do world.gravity opt.gravity
do world.time opt.time / 10
