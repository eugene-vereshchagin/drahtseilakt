help start = Choose which levels to play: the 13 originals, the featured maps, the community's. A level opens when you finish the one before it.
help highscore = The ten best scores of every level, with names and points. Left and Right change the level.
help options = Graphics, controls, sound, the menus' language and the extended mode with its extras and mods.
help credits = Who made the game, its music, sounds and textures, and the community maps.
help exit = Leaves the game, after one question. Your settings are already saved.
help back = Goes back to the previous page. Esc does the same.
help back pause = Closes the menu and goes on with the run.
help restart = Starts the level again from its beginning. In a run it asks first.
help exit level = Leaves the level for the main menu, after one question.

help level = Plays this level. Finish it to open the next one; with best times on, your fastest run is shown beside it.
help map = Plays this level. The star is its difficulty from 1 to 10; a question mark means unknown.
help graphics = Window size, screen sync, clouds, full screen and the Look of levels, and in the extended mode the field of view.
help controls = The keys that roll the ball, turn the camera and give the overview. Enter on a line waits for the new key.
help sound = The volume of music and sounds, and the sound theme: where the sounds and music come from.
help language = Chooses the language of the menus and texts.
help lang = Speaks the menus in this language at once and saves the choice. The gold one is spoken now.
help extended = The extended mode: extras beyond the original, packs for the look, the interface's source and mods. Off, the game is the original's.
help look original = Original: the textures of your copy of Ballance on the shapes of 2004, as the game was made.
help look remaster = Remaster: the toolbox's enlarged original textures on rounded shapes with relief. Physics, runs and records stay the same.
help look drahtseilakt = Drahtseilakt: our own textures on rounded shapes with relief, shipped with the game. The default.
help look player = A texture pack from your data folder (textures/) on rounded shapes; what it lacks comes from its fallback pack.
help look beautiful = The beautiful looks come after a level is imported with drahtseilakt-toolbox (import-maps, remaster); until then a level plays as it is.
note beautiful = Plays as it is. For the beautiful looks, import it with drahtseilakt-toolbox.
note toolbox = Original levels: import them with drahtseilakt-toolbox.
help resolution = The window's size (with exclusive full screen, the monitor's mode). Left and Right go through the sizes; it applies at once.
help synch yes = The frames wait for the monitor: no tearing, a little more delay.
help synch no = Frames are drawn as soon as they are ready: least delay, but the picture may tear. The original's default.
help clouds yes = The levels' cloud layer is drawn. How finely our sea of cloud is drawn is cloud-quality in settings.txt (low, medium, high).
help clouds no = No clouds are drawn: the sky stays plain, and our levels' sea of cloud is hidden too.
help fullscreen off = The game runs in a window of the size chosen above. F11 switches to full screen.
help fullscreen borderless = A borderless window over the whole desktop, at the desktop's own mode. F11 switches back to a window.
help fullscreen exclusive = The screen switches to the mode chosen above, where the system allows it. On Wayland it cannot and acts as borderless.
help models level = Each level is drawn with the parts it takes itself. The default. Models change only the look, never the physics. Without a copy of Ballance, these are our own parts and balls.
help models own = Drahtseilakt: Parts and balls drawn with our own shapes, in every level. Only the look changes, never the physics.
help models original = Ballance: Parts and balls drawn with the shapes of your own copy of Ballance. Only the look changes, never the physics.
help models player = A model pack from your data folder (models/): its shapes for parts and balls, the rest from its fallback. Only the look changes, never the physics.
help fov original = The camera's width across 4:3. This is the original's, 58 degrees; Left and Right change it from 40 to 100.
help fov wider = Wider than the original's 58 degrees: you see more to the sides, things look smaller. Left and Right change it from 40 to 100.
help fov narrower = Narrower than the original's 58 degrees: a closer view, things look bigger. Left and Right change it from 40 to 100.
help key forward = Rolls the ball away from the camera. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up. A key another action has is swapped.
help key backward = Rolls the ball towards the camera. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up. A key another action has is swapped.
help key left = Rolls the ball to the left. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up. A key another action has is swapped.
help key right = Rolls the ball to the right. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up. A key another action has is swapped.
help key rotation = Together with Left or Right it turns the camera around the ball. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up.
help key overview = Lifts the camera for a look over the level. Enter, then press the new key; Esc gives up.
help defaults = Back to the original's keys: the arrows, Shift and Space, and no inverted turn.
help invert yes = The camera turns the opposite way: Rotation with Left turns it to the right.
help invert no = The camera turns as in the original: Rotation with Left turns it to the left.
help music = The volume of the music, 0 (silent) to 10. Left and Right change it.
help effects = The volume of the sounds: rolling, hits, the fall, the menu's clicks. 0 (silent) to 10.
